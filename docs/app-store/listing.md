## English (U.S.)

**Subtitle** (App Information)

Mileage log & potni nalog

**Promotional text**

Your trips record themselves. Classify them as business, commute or private, and hand your accountant a monthly PDF logbook or potni nalog in a few taps.

**Keywords**

mileage,logbook,trip,tracker,business,commute,km,expense,potni nalog,car,reimbursement,receipt,drive

**Description**

MileLog keeps your car mileage log for you. It notices when you start driving, records the route and distance, and asks you afterwards whether the trip was business, commute or private. At the end of the month you get a clean PDF logbook for your accountant, including the Slovenian potni nalog.

AUTOMATIC OR MANUAL
• Automatic trip detection in the background: no need to open the app before every drive
• Recognises which car you are in from its Bluetooth audio connection
• Manual recording with one tap when you prefer to start and stop yourself
• Merge and edit trips; add customer, purpose and notes

BUILT FOR REIMBURSEMENT
• Business, commute and private trips kept apart
• Home and work addresses recognise commutes on their own
• Monthly PDF report for own-car business and commute trips
• Potni nalog for company cars, filled in with your company and driver details
• CSV export of all your trips

RECEIPTS AND MONTH-END
• Photograph fuel, parking and toll receipts; the text is read on your iPhone
• Month-end check flags missing details before you close a month
• Closed months are locked so reported trips stay as reported

YOUR DATA
• Trips sync to your MileLog account and are there on a new iPhone
• Data stored in the EU
• No ads, no third-party tracking
• Delete your account and all cloud data from Settings at any time

FREE AND PAID
MileLog Free includes manual recording, a monthly allowance of automatic trips, one vehicle, CSV export and all your history.

MileLog Paid adds unlimited automatic trips, monthly PDF reports and potni nalog, receipt photos, the month-end check and more than one vehicle. Paid is available as a monthly or yearly auto-renewable subscription, with a 14-day free trial for new subscribers.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the period. You can manage and cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID account settings. Any unused part of a free trial is forfeited when you buy a subscription.

## Slovenščina

**Podnaslov**

Potni nalog in evidenca km

**Promocijsko besedilo**

Vožnje se beležijo same. Razvrstite jih na službene, prevoz na delo ali zasebne in računovodji v nekaj dotikih pošljite mesečno PDF poročilo ali potni nalog.

**Ključne besede**

potni nalog,kilometrina,evidenca,vožnje,služben,km,avto,prevoz na delo,račun,stroški,poročilo

**Opis**

MileLog vodi evidenco prevoženih kilometrov namesto vas. Zazna, ko začnete voziti, zabeleži pot in razdaljo, nato pa vas vpraša, ali je bila vožnja službena, prevoz na delo ali zasebna. Ob koncu meseca dobite urejeno PDF poročilo za računovodjo, vključno s potnim nalogom.

SAMODEJNO ALI ROČNO
• Samodejno zaznavanje voženj v ozadju: aplikacije ni treba odpreti pred vsako vožnjo
• Prepozna avto po povezavi Bluetooth z avtoradiem
• Ročno beleženje z enim dotikom, kadar želite sami začeti in končati
• Združevanje in urejanje voženj; dodajte stranko, namen in opombe

ZA OBRAČUN KILOMETRINE
• Službene vožnje, prevoz na delo in zasebne vožnje ločeno
• Naslova doma in službe samodejno prepoznata prevoz na delo
• Mesečno PDF poročilo za službene vožnje in prevoz na delo z lastnim avtom
• Potni nalog za službeni avto z vašimi podatki o podjetju in vozniku
• Izvoz vseh voženj v CSV

RAČUNI IN KONEC MESECA
• Fotografirajte račune za gorivo, parkiranje in cestnino; besedilo se prebere na iPhonu
• Pregled ob koncu meseca opozori na manjkajoče podatke, preden mesec zaprete
• Zaprti meseci so zaklenjeni, da sporočene vožnje ostanejo nespremenjene

VAŠI PODATKI
• Vožnje se sinhronizirajo z vašim računom MileLog in so na voljo tudi na novem iPhonu
• Podatki so shranjeni v EU
• Brez oglasov in brez sledenja tretjih oseb
• Račun in vse podatke v oblaku lahko kadarkoli izbrišete v Nastavitvah

BREZPLAČNO IN PLAČLJIVO
MileLog Free vključuje ročno beleženje, mesečno kvoto samodejnih voženj, eno vozilo, izvoz CSV in vso zgodovino.

MileLog Paid doda neomejene samodejne vožnje, mesečna PDF poročila in potni nalog, fotografije računov, pregled ob koncu meseca in več vozil. Paid je na voljo kot mesečna ali letna samodejno obnovljiva naročnina, novi naročniki imajo 14 dni brezplačnega preizkusa.

Plačilo bremeni vaš Apple ID ob potrditvi nakupa. Naročnina se samodejno obnovi, razen če jo prekličete vsaj 24 ur pred koncem tekočega obdobja; obnovitev se zaračuna v 24 urah pred koncem obdobja. Naročnine upravljate in prekličete v nastavitvah računa Apple ID. Neporabljeni del brezplačnega preizkusa zapade ob nakupu naročnine.
